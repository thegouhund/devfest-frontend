# Login Surface Brief

## Scope & Visitor Mode
- Scope: Authentication surface (`src/components/Login.tsx`)
- Visitor Mode: Operate (Quick, trustworthy sign-in to family health dashboard)

## Audience, Job, Action & Content
- Audience: Existing Nadiku users (family admins, members, and standalone health monitors) returning to check vital trends.
- Job: Enter credentials securely and return to health telemetry without cognitive friction or alarm.
- Action: Validate email/password, submit login, provide one-click demo credentials for testing, route to onboarding if new.
- Proof/Content: Privacy assurance on rPPG processing, calm reassurance, clean feedback.

## Direction contract
### THESIS
The Calm Sanctuary portal: replaces sterile clinic portals and cold fintech login screens with a welcoming, tactile card that reassures users their family health data is private and securely encrypted.

### OWN-WORLD
Warm Sand canvas (#F6F4EE), elevated pure white card with ambient shadow, Obsidian Dark pill button (#0F172A), deep ink typography (#2B2622), and soft sage reassurance badges.

### STORY
The user arrives, recognizes the familiar warm branding, enters credentials or picks a 1-tap demo profile, and transitions smoothly into their family wellness space.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Clean centered container (max-w-md), prominent Nadiku logo & header, clear email/password inputs with visibility toggle, remember-me check, primary pill action, and quick-fill demo accounts.
